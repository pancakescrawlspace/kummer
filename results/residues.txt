(1) is section 6: over a local field the residue IS the invariant, so it
    must reproduce the Hilbert symbol -- and it does, with the sign
    (-1)^(v(a)v(b)) exactly accounting for the (-1/p)^(ab) in the classical
    formula.  (2) is the well-definedness of the symbol on K_2.  (3) is
    Faddeev's exact sequence: the last map is a sum of corestrictions, and
    reciprocity says the residues of a class on P^1 always cancel.  (4) is
    the compatibility that makes 'cor preserves unramified' true, tested on
    a degree-2 cover of the t-line ramified at 0 and infinity.  (5) is the
    mechanism by which descent functions give unramified algebras.  (6) is
    a Brauer-Manin obstruction found entirely by a residue argument, (7) is
    Faddeev used backwards to build classes to order, and (8) is Faddeev
    exactness on the left: no residues anywhere means constant.
