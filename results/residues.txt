residues.gp -- checks for residues.typ

The two worked examples of section 4.3, residues on P^1_Q:

  (3, t):
    P = t          kappa(P) = Q             residue = 3
    P = infinity   kappa(P) = Q             residue = 3
  (t, t-1):
    P = t          kappa(P) = Q             residue = -1
    P = t - 1      kappa(P) = Q             residue = 1
    P = infinity   kappa(P) = Q             residue = -1

  -- (3,t) is ramified exactly on (0) + (oo) with residue 3 at both;
     (t,t-1) is ramified exactly on (0) + (oo) with residue -1 at both,
     and the vanishing at t = 1 is the Steinberg relation.

checks

  (1) tame symbol = Hilbert symbol over Q_p, p odd : 0 wrong of 43200
  (2) Steinberg: every residue of (f, 1-f) is trivial : 0 wrong of 41
  (3) sum of corestrictions of the residues = 0 on P^1_Q : 0 wrong of 120
  (4) d_P . cor = sum_{w|P} cor . d_w  for Q(u) < Q(t), u = t^2 : 0 wrong of 330
  (5) div(f) 2-divisible => (f^2,g) unramified everywhere : 0 wrong of 433

(1) is section 6: over a local field the residue IS the invariant, so it
    must reproduce the Hilbert symbol -- and it does, with the sign
    (-1)^(v(a)v(b)) exactly accounting for the (-1/p)^(ab) in the classical
    formula.  (2) is the well-definedness of the symbol on K_2.  (3) is
    Faddeev's exact sequence: the last map is a sum of corestrictions, and
    reciprocity says the residues of a class on P^1 always cancel.  (4) is
    the compatibility that makes 'cor preserves unramified' true, tested on
    a degree-2 cover of the t-line ramified at 0 and infinity.  (5) is the
    mechanism by which descent functions give unramified algebras.
