Checks (1), (2) and (6) are the archimedean half of the method: the height
is a quadratic form, integral points are therefore gigantic, and gigantic
points have tiny elliptic logarithms.  Checks (3), (4), (5) and (7) are the
p-adic half, and they say the same thing in the other topology: a large
power of p in the denominator IS a large power of p dividing the p-adic
logarithm.  Baker's theorem, in each topology, says a non-zero linear form
in logarithms cannot be that small -- and the two estimates collide.
