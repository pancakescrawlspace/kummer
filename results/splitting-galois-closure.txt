  (3) control: the closure is not just any larger field
      K = Q(2^(1/3)), degree 3          |Spl(K)| = 1559
      L = closure of K, degree 6        |Spl(L)| = 1559,  Spl(K) minus Spl(L) = 0
      M = K(sqrt 7), degree 6, NOT the closure and not Galois:
        |Spl(M)| = 766,  Spl(K) minus Spl(M) = 793
      so K and M share no such equivalence; only the closure gives one

  (4) density of Spl(K) is 1/[L:Q], not 1/[K:Q]   (pi(100000) = 9592)

      x^4 - x - 1  0.03910    0.04167      0.25000      matches 1/[L:Q]

  (5) Spl depends only on the closure: quartic subfields of L
