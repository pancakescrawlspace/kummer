So for the minus curve Sha is trivial at every Fermat prime tested, and
the question 'what is the exponent of Sha' has the answer 'there is no
Sha'.  What grows instead is the Mordell-Weil group, and it grows for a
reason one can write on one line: p = m^2 + 1 with m a square.
