=========================================================================
 l-primary certificate for the single-place sweep, sections 3.3 and 3.4
=========================================================================

E : v^2 = u^3 + u + 1 (496a).   E_d : Y^2 = X^3 + d^2 X + d^3.

G := E^d(Q_p) is profinite abelian and topologically finitely generated,
so a closed subgroup R equals G as soon as R.Phi(G) = G, and for abelian
G,  G/Phi(G) = prod_l G/lG.  With G = Z_p x T only l = p and the primes
dividing #T contribute, and those factors have coprime orders.  Hence

   E^d(Q) dense in E^d(Q_p)  <=>  E^d(Q) --> E^d(Q_p)/l onto for each l,

checked one l at a time.  Each E^d(Q_p)/l is an F_l-space of dimension
<= 2, so a line is: a basis, the images of the generators of E^d(Q) in
it, and the rank of that matrix.  Generators are listed ONCE per (p,d).
